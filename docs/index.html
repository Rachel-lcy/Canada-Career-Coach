How requirements are classified

Requirements first, verdict second

Every requirement gets two labels: what the posting asks for, and where you stand.

Mandatory

Must be met by the start date. If your profile clearly doesn't meet it, it's a blocker. If it's unclear, it needs confirmation.

Pre-start

Mandatory, but usually done after an offer, like a police information check.

Flexible

Preferred, an asset, or offset by "or equivalent experience".

Try it: tap a phrase from a real Calgary posting

Built for Canada

More than pasting a posting into a chatbot.

Reads Canadian job requirements

Language levels, work authorization, security screening, licences and background checks, each sorted by level.

Reviews your resume for Canada

Letter-size format, Canadian spelling, and the personal details Canadian resumes leave out.

Limitations

  • Early version, tested by the maintainer only.
  • Only as good as the resume, profile and postings you give it.
  • Rules and wage data change. The coach cites sources and dates; check them.
  • The AI is instructed not to invent experience. That's an instruction, not a guarantee, so check every line.

Privacy and responsibility

  • Never put your profile or resumes in a public repository, and never share your personalized copy of the skill.
  • Not career, legal or immigration advice. Check permit rules with IRCC.
  • Independent project. Not affiliated with Anthropic, Google or the Government of Canada.
