01

Personalize it

Your facts, kept on your side

Set it up once, in three steps

  1. Upload your resume in a chat where the skill is installed.
  2. Paste this prompt and send it:
    Set up canada-career-coach for me from my resume. Fill references/profile-template.md and save it as references/my-profile.md. Ask me about anything missing. Don't invent anything, and show me every line before you save.
  3. Answer the AI's questions and check every line before you say yes.

Done. Your profile is saved only in your own copy of the skill, and every later chat uses it.

The AI may ask you about

Target roles Skills you can prove Paid / volunteer / project Availability Work authorization (optional) Pay floor (optional)

Two to four resume versions

Each version aims at one role family and says what it does not claim. The coach picks the best base for every posting.

Web DeveloperIT SupportBusiness AnalysisProject Coordination

Never asked: SIN, permit number, passport number or criminal history.

02

Assess a posting

Requirements first, verdict second

Every assessment returns five sections

ARequirementsEach one quoted, given a level and a status
BRole fitDuties, sector and seniority, not just the title
CKeywordsUp to 15 terms: supported, partly, or no evidence
DResumeWhich version to send and why
ESalaryHourly, bi-weekly or annual, compared on one basis

Three levels of requirement

Hard blocker

Mandatory and can't be met before the start date.

"Canadian citizens only" · a regulated licence you don't hold

Pre-start condition

Mandatory, but usually obtained after an offer.

Police information check · Reliability Status

Flexible

Preferred, an asset, or offset by an equivalency.

"French an asset" · "degree or equivalent experience"

Try it: what level is this requirement?

These phrases come from real Calgary postings. Tap one to see how the coach reads it.

Hard blocker

Driving is part of the job. If you don't hold a Class 5 licence now, this is a blocker, not a to-do item.

03

Tailor your resume

Before and after, never a rewrite

Keep the scope honest

The coach mirrors the posting's words only where you have the experience, and keeps a home lab a home lab.

Canadian resume checks

1–2 pages, letter sizeNo photoNo date of birth No marital statusNo SINCanadian spellingReferences on request
BeforeManaged the company's Active Directory and user accounts.

Reads as production work. The profile says this was a home lab.

AfterConfigured Active Directory users, groups and policies in a Windows Server home lab.

Same skill, true scope, and the keyword is still there.

04

Write it yourself

AI audits, you author

Cover letter: three questions first

Which organization and role? What genuinely interests you? Which experience do you want to lead with? Then you get an outline with prompts. Full prose only if you ask, labelled as a draft.

  1. Opening with a point of view
  2. How you work
  3. Evidence with results
  4. Why this organization
  5. Short, confident close

Proofreading

Paste any resume or letter. Edits come back as before-and-after pairs.

Canadian spellingTensePassive voice "Responsible for"Vague numbersDate formats Titles match across documents

The principle behind the project

Built for Canada

Adapted, not copied.

This project adapts UK Career Coach by NotXinrui. The principles stayed the same. The market rules, the vocabulary and the examples changed.

UK versionCanada version
CV, two pages, A4Resume, one to two pages, letter size
Right to work, visa sponsorshipWork authorization: permits and LMIA, as you state them
SC / DV clearanceSecurity screening: Reliability, Enhanced Reliability, Secret, Top Secret
Blockers: yes or noThree levels: hard blockers, pre-start conditions, flexible requirements
No language checkOfficial languages: English, French and federal levels such as BBB
British spellingCanadian spelling: colour, centre, organize
UK salary sourcesJob Bank, provincial surveys (alis in Alberta) and public-sector pay grids
PMO and consulting keywordsWeb development, IT support, project coordination and business analysis
One national marketProvincial notes, with Alberta and Calgary as the worked example

Limitations

  • Early version, tested by the maintainer only.
  • Only as good as the resume, profile and postings you give it.
  • Rules and wage data change. The coach cites sources and dates; check them.
  • Only Alberta has detailed provincial notes so far. Contributions are welcome.

Privacy and responsibility

  • Never put your filled-in profile or resumes in a public repository, and never share your personalized copy.
  • What you paste or upload is processed by your AI provider. Remove your phone number and address first.
  • Not career, legal or immigration advice. Check permit rules with IRCC.
  • Independent project. Not affiliated with Anthropic, Google or the Government of Canada.
